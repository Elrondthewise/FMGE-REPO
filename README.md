# FMGE Anki Deck

This deck was created over time through the combined efforts of many medical students, FMGE aspirants, interns, and doctors. The cards included here have been written, rewritten, contributed, donated, collected, and refined from multiple sources and experiences, with the goal of helping others study more effectively.

This is a community-driven educational resource and is not the work of any single individual claiming ownership over all content. If you recognize material similar to your own contributions, know that it was shared in good faith as part of collaborative learning.

Every effort has been made to keep the information accurate, high-yield, and useful for revision. However, medicine is vast, humans are flawed, and Anki decks assembled at 2 AM before exams are not exactly peer-reviewed journals. There may still be occasional mistakes, outdated facts, typographical errors, or ambiguities.

If you find any errors, please understand that they are entirely unintentional. Constructive corrections and improvements are always appreciated.

This deck is intended purely for educational and personal learning purposes.

Please do not:

Sell this deck
Reupload modified versions while claiming sole ownership
Misrepresent community contributions as your own original work

If this deck helps you, consider improving it further and helping someone else in the same way. That is usually how the best medical resources survive.

Good luck with your preparation.
